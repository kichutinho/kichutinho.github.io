🎨 Visual Insights

What does a "constellation" mean here? The app reads your connections list and groups them by their current job titles. Each group becomes a colored cluster. Lines show who might know each other via mutual connections, creating a stunning web-like star field.

Colors are assigned automatically: blue for tech, green for marketing, orange for health, etc. You can hover over any node to see the person's name, role, and industry. Click a cluster to see a summary of that profession group.

🧰 Troubleshooting & FAQ

Q: Do I need to install any libraries?

A: No. It runs entirely in your browser using modern web technologies.

Q: Is my data safe?

Absolutely. No servers receive your information. Everything is processed locally in your browser. You can even disconnect from the internet after the app loads.

Q: How do I export my LinkedIn data?

On LinkedIn: click "Me" → "Settings & Privacy" → "Data Privacy" → "Get a copy of your data". Request the "Connections" dataset. LinkedIn emails you a .zip; inside that, you'll find a Connections.csv file.

Q: The map looks empty?

Make sure your CSV file contains at least 10 connections. Also check the file isn't corrupted — try opening it in software like Excel to preview.
